01 / SAFER-GAMBLING INTERVENTION & EVALUATION

FortuneFlo

Change what happens to the money, not the game.

FortuneFlo explores what should happen after gambling risk is recognised: which intervention may be appropriate, when to act and how to evaluate what follows. The broader architecture is a development design; its full intervention catalogue is not implemented or validated.

BUILT FAKE-MONEY SIMULATOR · OPERATOR EFFECTIVENESS UNVALIDATED

The implemented simulator explores money-handling as one intervention example, with a transaction record and balance reconciliation. Simulation can test defined behaviour; it cannot establish real-world harm reduction.

Built work, limits & the next proof question

Built in a separate internal development project: fake-money transactions, an event ledger and balance reconciliation. Historical development tests and simulations are internal evidence, not independent validation. No live casino, customer funds or operator data are connected to this demonstration.

One useful challenge: for an existing intervention, what evidence would distinguish a meaningful change from an imposed restriction or missing activity, and show whether further action is needed? The review must retain adverse outcomes and causal limits.

The next step is to agree proof criteria with relevant specialists, then assess data readiness and authorised shadow evaluation before considering any controlled prospective test. This is an evaluation route, not a claim of effectiveness, regulatory approval or endorsement. Public material excludes confidential decision rules and implementation detail.

02 / PERSONALISED DARTS COACHING

Double Infinity

Your personal AI darts coach. Built to turn throw data into practical observations, focused practice and a coaching experience shaped around the player rather than generic advice.

BUILT DEVELOPMENT PROTOTYPE · END-TO-END ACCEPTANCE OPEN

The current development work covers player onboarding, match review and tailored practice. Scolia is a source of throw data, not the product's name or an endorsed partner.

Built work, limitations & next proof

Coaching screens, throw-data handling and automated test loops exist. The complete live, signed-in coaching journey, accuracy of every event and historical import, and measurable coaching improvements remain subject to further acceptance and independent evaluation.

Development contact with Scolia does not imply an official partnership, endorsement, product approval or independently established coaching effectiveness.
